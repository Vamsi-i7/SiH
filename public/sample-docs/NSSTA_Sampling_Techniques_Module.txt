CURRICULUM MODULE: OFFICIAL STATISTICS & SAMPLE SURVEY METHODOLOGY
SUBORDINATE STATISTICAL SERVICE (SSS) INDUCTION TRAINING

MODULE 1: PRINCIPLES OF LARGE-SCALE SURVEY DESIGN
1.1 Sampling Errors vs Non-Sampling Errors: Sampling error results from measuring a probability subset rather than the complete population. Non-sampling error arises from response bias, interviewer misunderstanding, data capture errors, or flawed frames.
1.2 Design Effect (DEFF): Defined as the ratio of the variance of an estimator under the chosen complex multi-stage design to the variance under Simple Random Sampling (SRS) of the same sample size.
1.3 Stratification Gain: Stratification maximizes between-strata variance while minimizing within-stratum variance, yielding lower standard errors for key macroeconomic indicators.

MODULE 2: DESK SCRUTINY OF FIELD SCHEDULES
2.1 Primary Function of JSO Scrutiny: Scrutiny verifies logical consistency across related schedules before microdata aggregation.
2.2 Range and Consistency Checks:
- In Annual Survey of Industries (ASI): Net Sale Value = Gross Sale Value - Taxes - Rebates. Gross sale value cannot be lower than Net sale value.
- In Household Consumption Expenditure Survey (HCES): Monthly per-capita expenditure must be consistent with reported earnings and transfers.
2.3 Imputation Guidelines: Hot-deck imputation replaces item non-responses using donor units possessing similar demographic profiles within the same geographical stratum.

MODULE 3: NATIONAL ACCOUNTS STATISTICS (NAS) & AGGREGATE ESTIMATION
3.1 Gross Fixed Capital Formation (GFCF): Measures acquisitions less disposals of produced fixed assets during the accounting period.
3.2 Consumer Price Index (CPI): Base year basket weights are periodically re-benchmarked using nationwide NSS Consumer Expenditure rounds.
