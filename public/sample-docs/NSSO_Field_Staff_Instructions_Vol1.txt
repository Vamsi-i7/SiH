NATIONAL SAMPLE SURVEY OFFICE (FIELD OPERATIONS DIVISION)

INSTRUCTIONS TO FIELD STAFF — VOLUME I
SURVEY DESIGN, CONCEPTS, DEFINITIONS AND PROCEDURES
(78TH ROUND / PLFS SURVEY GUIDELINES)

CHAPTER 1: GENERAL DESCRIPTION AND COVERAGE
1.1 The National Sample Survey (NSS), set up by the Government of India in 1950 to collect socio-economic data employing scientific sampling methods, conducts nationwide multi-subject household and enterprise surveys.
1.2 The Field Operations Division (FOD) with its headquarters at New Delhi and Faridabad and a network of Zonal, Regional, and Sub-Regional offices across India is responsible for the collection of primary field data.

CHAPTER 2: CONCEPTS, DEFINITIONS AND OPERATIONAL PROCEDURES
2.1 First Stage Unit (FSU): In rural areas, the FSU is the 2011 Census village. In urban areas, it is the Urban Frame Survey (UFS) block.
2.2 Hamlet-Group (hg) / Sub-Block (sb) Formation: When the approximate present population of a sample village or UFS block is 1,200 or more, it is divided into a suitable number of sub-divisions called hamlet-groups in rural areas and sub-blocks in urban areas.
2.3 Rule of Equal Size: Hamlet-groups must be of approximately equal population size, demarcated using identifiable natural or permanent man-made boundaries (roads, canals, railway tracks).
2.4 Household Definition: A group of persons normally living together and taking food from a common kitchen constitutes a household. Temporary visitors (staying < 6 months) are excluded from listing to avoid double-counting.

CHAPTER 3: SCHEDULE 0.0 — LIST OF HOUSEHOLDS AND SAMPLING SELECTION
3.1 Path of Listing: Enumeration must begin from the North-West corner of the FSU and proceed in a clockwise or continuous serpentine sweep to ensure complete coverage without omission or duplication.
3.2 Second Stage Stratum (SSS): Listed households are categorized into relatively homogeneous socio-economic strata (SSS) based on household characteristics or consumption patterns before circular systematic sampling is executed.
3.3 Non-Response Protocol: If a sample household is temporarily absent, at least three revisit attempts must be recorded before seeking supervisor authorization for substitution. Casual replacement without documented attempts is strictly prohibited under survey protocol.

CHAPTER 4: CAPI TABLET FIELD PROTOCOLS
4.1 Offline Ingestion: Completed interview forms are encrypted and cached in local SQLite storage. All coordinates must achieve a GPS accuracy threshold within ±10 metres with 4 active satellite locks.
4.2 Soft Warning vs Hard Error: Soft warnings permit continuation with mandatory supervisory remarks; hard errors block questionnaire progression until inconsistencies are resolved.
4.3 Data Integrity: SHA-256 cryptographic hashes are computed locally upon form completion and verified upon upload to the regional server.
